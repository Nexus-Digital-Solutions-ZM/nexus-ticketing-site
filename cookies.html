Cookie Policy

Nexus Digital Solutions · Last updated 25 November 2026

1. The short version

The Nexus Ticketing website at nexusticketing.online does not set cookies. We count visits with a cookie-free analytics tool, and we do not use advertising or tracking cookies.

This site is run by Nexus Digital Solutions, a company registered with PACRA in Lusaka, Zambia. The shop and admin apps at app.nexusticketing.online use only the cookies needed to sign you in and keep your session secure. A cookie is a small text file a website saves on your device.

2. What this site does use

ServiceWhy we use itCookies?What it receives
Vercel Web Analytics Counts page views so we know which parts of the site are read No Page visited, referring site, country, browser and device type. It does not follow you across sites.
Google Fonts Loads the Courier Prime font where Courier New is missing No Your browser asks Google's servers for the font file, so Google receives your IP address and browser details.

We do not run advertising pixels, social media trackers or embedded videos on this site.

3. Your choices

You can block or delete cookies, and block third-party requests such as fonts, in your browser settings. The site works either way.

If we ever add cookies that are not strictly needed, we will update this policy and ask for your consent before setting them.

4. Your rights and contact

Under the Data Protection Act, 2021 of Zambia you can ask to see the personal data we hold about you, correct it, object to its use, or ask us to delete it. You can also complain to the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner.

Changes. We will update this policy if the way the site works changes, and show the new date at the top.
