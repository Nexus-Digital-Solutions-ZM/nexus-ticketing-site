Why organisers switch

Every gate has the same three problems

Paper stubs, screenshots and cash boxes work until the crowd is bigger than the plan.

Fake and shared tickets

A photo of a ticket looks like a ticket. At a busy gate nobody can tell the original from the copy.

With NexusEach ticket has its own code. The second scan of the same code is refused.

Networks that fail

Venue signal drops exactly when the queue is longest, and every online-only scanner stops with it.

With NexusThe ticket list is on the phone before doors open. Scans are stored and synced when signal returns.

Money that goes missing

Cash at the counter, sales on phones and people through the gate rarely add up on the same night.

With NexusSales, scans and till totals sit in one report, so a gap shows up as a number, not a rumour.
How it works

From setup to closing time

1
Before the event

We set it up with you

Venue and gates, ticket categories and prices, sale dates. Your team gets back-office logins with the right roles.

2
On the day

Staff scan, fans walk in

Gate staff scan on an Android phone. Green means go, red means stop. Signal can come and go.

3
After the event

You get the numbers

Sold, admitted, no-shows, duplicates and till totals by gate and by staff member, as a PDF or CSV.
